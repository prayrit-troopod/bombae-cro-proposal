02 · Focus journey

What we found between Add to Cart and Order

We tested the live journey on a phone. Every product page shares the same theme, cart and checkout, so each issue below applies across the store. Screenshots are from the Fuzzoff 6-in-1 page, Bombae's second-highest revenue product. Select an issue to see the detail.

01

Add to Cart has no next step

Product page → Cart
What happens
  • After Add to Cart, a small "Product added to cart" message shows for about 2 seconds. It can't be tapped.
  • Both Add to Cart buttons turn into a "− 1 +" quantity selector. No cart opens and no Checkout button appears.
  • The scroll-to-top button sits on top of the "+" on the sticky bar.
Impact

To continue, shoppers have to find the small bag icon at the top right. On the Hairess page, about 7,700 Add to Cart taps led to only 507 taps on the cart icon. Store-wide, 45% of carts never start checkout.

02

Returning shoppers see no buy button

Product page, item already in cart
What happens

When a product is already in the cart, for example for a shopper returning from a retargeting ad, every buy button on its page shows only "− 1 +". There is no Add to Cart, Buy Now or Checkout button.

Impact

Shoppers who come back to complete a purchase are the most likely to buy, and the page gives them nothing to tap that moves them forward.

03

The cart drawer pulls attention away from checkout

Cart
What happens
  • An open coupon code field and "View all offers (8)" sit at the top of the cart, above everything else.
  • The cart says "You have unlocked all rewards!" while the announcement bar promises a free tote above ₹2,500.
  • The add-on product cards are cut off, so their names can't be read. On Hairess, the cart suggests body wax strips instead of the matching Heat Protectant.
  • The prepaid incentive is ₹20 off UPI.
Impact

A coupon field at the top sends shoppers away to look for codes, and some don't come back. Unclear rewards and add-ons remove reasons to complete the order or add one more item.

04

Most checkouts don't complete, and the reason isn't visible yet

Checkout (GoKwik)
What happens

9,537 of 39,286 started checkouts become orders (24.3%). Every step, from phone number and OTP to address and payment, runs inside GoKwik and isn't visible in GA4.

Impact

This is the largest drop in the store funnel. The re-audit will map each checkout step using GoKwik's data, so the fixes are based on where shoppers actually leave.

03 · Approach and roadmap

We rebuild the Add to Cart to Order journey, test it against the live theme on a smaller audience, then roll it out to all traffic and keep improving it. Once that journey is optimised, we move to the next one, so each quarter has a clear focus.
